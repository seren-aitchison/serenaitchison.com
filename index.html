The combination

Two disciplines, deliberately.

A computer scientist can build a machine learning pipeline without the chemical intuition to judge what its output means. A medicinal chemist can reason about molecular interactions without the engineering to work at the scale biological data now demands.

  • Chemistry first. Three years of synthesis, characterisation and molecular design, including a 40-credit research dissertation involving catalysis, analytical characterisation and docking.
  • Computation second, and deliberately. Genomic annotation, population genetics, statistical genetics and machine learning, built project by project against real public APIs.
  • Engineering throughout. Two production systems with databases, sandboxed execution, automated testing and continuous deployment.
  • Honesty as a method. Every project states what its data can and cannot support. Several exist specifically to document where a method breaks.
