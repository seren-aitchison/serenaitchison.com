Computational Lab · interactive

Explore the science yourself.

Five working tools, not screenshots. Three of them query public scientific APIs live from your browser, so you are looking at current data. Nothing you do here is uploaded, stored or tracked.

Pharmacogenomics explorer Population frequencies Molecular explorer Interaction networks Statistics studio
Educational tools. These are built for exploring methods and public data. Nothing here is clinical advice, a diagnostic result, or a basis for any decision about medicines.

01 · Pharmacogenomics explorer

Variant annotation from Ensembl, allele function and drug association from CPIC.

ReadyThe project
Variant

Live lookup against the Ensembl REST API (GRCh38). The allele function and drug columns are curated from CPIC.

02 · Population frequency explorer

Allele frequencies across gnomAD population groups, fetched live.

ReadyThe project

Population labels are broad continental groupings. A frequency is not a risk, and these labels do not describe any individual.

03 · Molecular explorer

Structures in 3D with descriptors and drug-likeness rules, from PubChem.

ReadyThe proposal

Descriptors are PubChem's computed properties. Rule filters are heuristics for oral bioavailability, not predictions of activity.

04 · Protein interaction network

Build a network around a protein, then watch the hubs move as you raise the confidence threshold.

ReadyThe proposal

05 · Statistics studio

Manhattan and QQ plots, and expression fold changes, computed in the page.

Local computationThe project

GWAS data here is simulated, as in the source projects. The expression values are the illustrative dataset from the differential expression workflow.

How these are built

No framework, no build step.

Every tool is plain ES modules, SVG and canvas. Network requests go straight to the public APIs from your browser. The force layout, the plots and the statistics are implemented here rather than pulled from a charting library, because the point is to show the working.
