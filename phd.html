Research thinking

The habits I'd bring to a group.

These are visible in the published work, which is the only reason I am willing to claim them.

  • I report the result that undermines the method. The VEP project exists largely because SIFT calls a well-established functional variant "tolerated". That is the interesting part, and it is in the findings rather than buried.
  • I check against a case whose answer I know. The gene annotation pipeline silently returned "no overlapping gene" for a variant that is textbook MTHFR. It was only caught because the right answer was known in advance.
  • I separate the layers of a claim. Transcript annotation, allele function and clinical guidance are three different kinds of evidence. Collapsing them is how pharmacogenomic overclaims happen.
  • I state what the data cannot support. A fold-change analysis with one value per condition gets no p-value, and the project says so rather than quietly implying significance.
